Who we are

A society built around the power of education.

Nara Educational Society (NES) is an educational and youth-development initiative committed to helping young people learn, build confidence, develop useful skills and contribute positively to their communities.

We bring together students, educators, professionals and volunteers to turn ideas into meaningful educational experiences.

  • Student-centered learning and guidance
  • Leadership and practical skill development
  • Community participation and collaboration
