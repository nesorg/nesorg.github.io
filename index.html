About NES

Who we are and what we stand for.

Education is more than a classroom

Nara Educational Society is an educational and youth-development initiative focused on academic opportunity, practical skills, leadership and social responsibility. By connecting students, educators, professionals and volunteers, NES builds a supportive environment for learning and growth.

🎯 Our Vision

An educated, empowered, skilled and socially responsible generation that contributes to a progressive society.

✦ Our Mission

To promote educational opportunities, nurture young talent, strengthen leadership and support career readiness.

Our values

📖 Knowledge

Continuous learning and curiosity.

🛡 Integrity

Honesty and accountability.

🤲 Inclusivity

Respect for every background.

💡 Innovation

Creative thinking and better solutions.

🤝 Collaboration

Working together with shared purpose.

♥ Service

Giving time and skills for others.
