About the Society

Who we are

Nara Educational Society (NES) is a community-driven non-profit based in the Nara desert of Sindh, Pakistan. We work to lift the educational standards and self-confidence of young people growing up in remote villages.

Through regional talent hunts, academic assessments and competitions, we give rural students a stage to show what they can do.

Read Our Story →

Our core goal

  • Promote language fluency among students
  • Foster healthy academic competition
  • Build confidence in underserved rural students
  • Celebrate every position holder at their own school

Founder & Chief Patron

Chief Patron

Niaz Ali

Niaz Ali is the Chief Patron and guiding force behind Nara Educational Society. He believes that every child in the Nara desert deserves the same chance to learn, speak with confidence and dream big, and he supports the society's talent hunts, NES Tests and award ceremonies.
