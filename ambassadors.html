The NES University Ambassador Program is designed to connect motivated students across educational institutions.

University ambassadors

Be a voice for education and positive change.

NES University Ambassadors act as a bridge between their campuses and the society. They help share verified educational announcements, encourage student participation and support local activities.

  • Represent NES responsibly at your institution
  • Support official program and event announcements
  • Develop leadership and communication experience
  • Connect students with educational opportunities
