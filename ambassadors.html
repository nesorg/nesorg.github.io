NES University Ambassador Program

Students who carry the message of Nara Educational Society to their universities and institutions.

What ambassadors do

  • Represent NES at their university or institution
  • Share announcements, events and opportunities with fellow students
  • Help organise and promote NES programs
  • Bring new volunteers and members to the society

Who can apply

  • University or college students
  • Interested in education and community service
  • Good communication skills and a sense of responsibility
  • Willing to give regular time to NES activities

How to apply

Apply

Fill in the registration form and choose University Ambassador.

Review

The NES team reviews applications and contacts shortlisted students.

Welcome

Selected ambassadors are introduced to the team and receive their responsibilities.

Ambassador directory

The list of NES ambassadors, with their universities and photographs, will be published here as ambassadors join the program.
