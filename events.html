Our Events

Talent hunts, NES Tests, competitions and award ceremonies that bring learning, confidence and celebration to the Nara desert.

What we organise

Regional Talent Hunt Programs

Open programs that search for talented students in remote villages and give them a platform to be seen and encouraged.

TalentRegionalOpen to schools

NES Tests

Regional academic assessment evaluations that measure learning, build healthy competition and help recognise hard-working students.

AcademicAssessmentRegional

Spelling Bees

Lively spelling competitions that strengthen vocabulary, language fluency and the courage to speak in front of others.

LanguageCompetition

Student Competitions

Friendly academic contests between students and schools that make learning exciting and promote healthy academic competition.

CompetitionSchools

Award Ceremonies

Ceremonies held at regional schools and colleges to celebrate position holders in front of their teachers, classmates and families.

How an event works

Announcement

The date, place and rules are shared on our Announcements page and WhatsApp.

Registration

Students register through their school or by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Test or competition

Students take part in an NES Test, spelling bee or talent hunt round.

Celebration

Position holders receive recognition at an award ceremony.
