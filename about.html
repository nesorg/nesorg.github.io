About Nara Educational Society

A community-driven non-profit working to boost the educational standards and confidence of youth in the remote Nara desert.

Our story

Children in the villages of the Nara desert often study far from big cities and with few chances to take part in competitions or assessments. Nara Educational Society was formed by people from the community to change that.

Today the society arranges regional talent hunt programs, regional academic assessment evaluations (NES Tests) and student competitions, and visits local schools and colleges to honour position holders.

Our mission

To promote language fluency, encourage healthy academic competition and build confidence among underserved rural students, so that every child in Nara can believe in a brighter future.

Our motto: Education builds a brighter future.

Our Core Goals

Language fluency

Spelling bees and speaking activities help students read, spell and express themselves with confidence.

Healthy competition

Fair tests and contests encourage students to work hard, aim high and respect each other.

Confidence for rural students

Recognition at their own schools tells underserved students that their effort matters.

What We Do

  • Regional talent hunt programs
  • Regional academic assessment evaluations (NES Tests)
  • Student competitions, including spelling bees
  • Award ceremonies that celebrate position holders

Where we work

Our work is centred on the Nara desert area of Sindh, Pakistan. We organise events right at village schools and local colleges, such as:

  • Iqra Public School, Village Allah Bux Jamro
  • Government Degree College Choondiko

Founder & Chief Patron

Chief Patron

Niaz Ali

As Chief Patron, Niaz Ali guides the vision of the society and stands behind every effort to raise educational standards in the remote Nara desert. His patronage gives students, teachers and families the encouragement to keep learning, and keeps the society focused on its goal of language fluency, healthy academic competition and confidence for underserved rural students.
