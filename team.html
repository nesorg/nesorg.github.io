Meet Our Team

The people who lead Nara Educational Society and work every day for the students of the Nara desert.

Chief Patron

Niaz Ali

As Chief Patron, Niaz Ali guides the vision of the society and stands behind every effort to raise educational standards in the remote Nara desert. His patronage gives students, teachers and families the encouragement to keep learning, and keeps the society focused on its goal of language fluency, healthy academic competition and confidence for underserved rural students.

Message the Society
President

Ghulam Hussain

As President, Ghulam Hussain leads the society's work on the ground. He coordinates with schools, colleges and community members to organise talent hunts, NES Tests, spelling bees and award ceremonies, and makes sure every programme reaches the students who need it most.

Message the Society
General Secretary

Sajid Ali

As General Secretary, Sajid Ali looks after the society's communication, records and event planning. He is the point of contact for schools, partners and families, and helps keep announcements, schedules and results organised and on time.
