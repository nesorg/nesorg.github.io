How NES handles your information.

Important information about applications, communication, personal data and participation.

Privacy notice

Nara Educational Society collects information submitted through its membership, ambassador, event and contact forms for the purpose of reviewing requests, administering activities and communicating with applicants.

Information we collect

Depending on the form, information may include your name, email, phone number, city, educational institution, qualification, academic department, application details and participation information.

How information is used

  • To process applications and inquiries.
  • To coordinate activities and respond to participants.
  • To maintain internal administrative records and, where applicable, issue certificates.
  • To share program updates when separately permitted.

Public profiles

Ambassador or team profile details will only be made public after approval and appropriate consent. Public certificate verification reveals only limited certificate fields.

Data protection

Access to private applications is restricted to authorized administrators. NES should maintain secure access controls, backups, and retention procedures appropriate to its activities.

Your choices

Applicants may contact NES to request corrections or discuss removal of information, subject to applicable recordkeeping needs.

Participation terms

Submitting an application does not guarantee membership, appointment, event acceptance, scholarship funding, or a certificate. Published opportunity links may lead to third-party sites with their own terms.

Program dates, participation criteria and benefits will be governed by the corresponding official announcements. Respectful conduct and accurate submitted information are expected.

Contact

For questions about personal information, use the NES contact form. NES leadership should review and approve this draft policy before accepting real applications.
