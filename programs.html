Flagship concept

Nara Talent Hunt

The Nara Talent Hunt initiative aims to identify and encourage academically talented students through fair competitions, recognition and educational support.

  • Academic tests and quizzes
  • Age-appropriate participation categories
  • Recognition and certificates
  • Transparent event information and eligibility
Registration, test schedules and awards are subject to an official program announcement.
